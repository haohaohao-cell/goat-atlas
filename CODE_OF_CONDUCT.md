# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want GOAT Atlas to be a place where fans can disagree about football without
attacking each other. Everyone is welcome regardless of age, nationality,
gender, identity, experience level, or favorite player.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Critique the data or the argument, not the person.
- Bring sources when correcting a fact.
- Accept that “greatest” is an opinion; the project documents evidence, not
  declares a winner.
- Respect copyright and personal privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Spam, doxxing, or sharing private information.
- Uploading pirated broadcasts, unauthorized photos, or club/competition
  logos.
- Repeatedly reopening the same factual dispute without new evidence.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block contributors
who violate these rules. Reports can be sent to the maintainers through a
private GitHub issue or the contact method listed on the repository profile.

This is a lightweight code of conduct, not a legal document. It is inspired by
the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.
